2. More source sites worth pointing Tool 2 at (published addresses)

  • Google Play / app store developer pages - developer contact emails are published by design.
  • Conference, meetup and podcast speaker pages - often list a direct email.
  • Personal sites, blogs, resumes, Linktree-style pages - founders and senior people often publish theirs.
  • Company docs, support portals, security.txt files - security@ and support@ are published by convention.
  • Regulatory filings (MCA, SEC, EU registries) - officer emails sometimes appear in PDFs.
  • WHOIS / RDAP - mostly redacted since GDPR; occasionally shows a contact for small domains.

Honest limits: raw SMTP mailbox probing cannot run on Cloudflare Workers or Vercel (outbound port 25 is blocked), so true mailbox verification comes from the keyed APIs above; the Verified label is reserved for that. MX records cannot detect catch-all domains. Pattern-generated addresses are labeled Unverified guess, and no candidate is presented as the best email without address-specific evidence. Gravatar/PGP hits are labeled Seen publicly (past public use, not current deliverability). The app stores nothing, but checks do query outside services: domain to a public DNS resolver, company name to Clearbit, hashed candidates to Gravatar, candidates to the PGP keyserver, the name to GitHub search, and scanned page URLs to a reader relay; private/internal or credentialed URLs are refused. LinkedIn itself stays off-limits: they block scraping hard and it gets tools banned.
